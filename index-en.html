What is TÖYAK®?

TÖYAK stands for Facility Scaling, Management, and Analysis Criteria. It is a proprietary corporate health analysis and evaluation matrix.

TÖYAK is an original management model designed to automate processes in corporate structures and an operational workflow representing engineering algorithms:

  • Diagnosis (T): X-raying field conditions (construction, factories, or plazas) to expose leakages and budget drifts.
  • Measurement (Ö): Mathematically scoring losses (OEE, waste rates, quantity deviations) and root-cause analysis.
  • Management (Y): Transitioning processes from individual memory to Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and autonomous systems.
  • Analysis (A): Instant monitoring of corporate-field alignment via dynamic dashboards and Corporate Health Indexes.
  • Control (K): Guaranteeing quality and financial safety through SLA specifications integrated into subcontractor contracts.
